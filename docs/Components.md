Components

-Arduino uno
-Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor v1.2 (Root Zone Moisture)
-HW-080 Moisture Sensor (Surface Water Level / Standing Water Ponding Probe)
-DC Water Pump
-Buck Converter
-Relay Module
-3x 18650 Battery Holder Series and 3x 18650 Batteries
-Solar Charger Module
-30W Solar Panel


The system is designed to automate the watering of miniature rice fields it has a solar panel that charges the batteries during the day and a relay module that controls the water pump to irrigate the miniature rice fields based on soil moisture levels. The buck converter is used to regulate the voltage from the batteries to power the Arduino Uno and other components. it has 2 soil sensor right? the 1st one (Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor v1.2) is placed at the root zone to check the soil mosture  while the 2nd one is placed at the surface to measure the Water level content.
