⚡ Star Wiring & Presentation Mode Architecture

Main Power: 12V DC Power Adapter + 5A Step-Down Module (Fixed 5.00V) powers the entire system with unlimited runtime.
Isolated Motor Current: Motor return current flows strictly through Breadboard Row 20 & 25 back to Star GND, completely isolating the Arduino ATmega328P microcontroller from motor noise and inductive spikes.
USB Connection: Used only as a Serial Data Bridge (115200 baud) for live evaluation telemetry.

Step-by-Step Presentation Procedure
  1. Plug In 12V Power Adapter: Plug the adapter into the wall and connect the barrel plug directly into the module's DC jack.
  2. Connect USB Data Cable: Connect the Arduino Uno to your laptop/PC and open the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud.
  3. Test Soil Sensor (Air vs Soil/Water): Hold the capacitive sensor probe in air (0%) and then dip it into moist soil or a cup of water (70–80%).
  4. Test HW-080 Water Level Sensor: Submerge the probe in water and observe the live ASCII gauge bar fill up on screen in real time.
  5. Demonstrate Automated Pump Trigger: Switch to Auto Mode (A) and lift the sensor out of the water to demonstrate automated pump refilling.
