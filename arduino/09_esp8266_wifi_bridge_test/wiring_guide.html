⚡ Golden Rules for Error-Free Assembly

  • 1. Never Connect Arduino 5V TX Directly to ESP8266 RX: The ESP8266 silicon pins are strictly 3.3V rated. Always route Arduino Pin D10 through the 1kΩ / 2kΩ voltage divider into NodeMCU Pin D1.
  • 2. Star Ground Bus Is Mandatory: Both Arduino GND and NodeMCU GND must terminate on the central breadboard blue ground bus. Without this common reference, UART serial bytes will arrive corrupted.
  • 3. Feed Power into Vin, Never 3V3: The LM2596 output is 5.00V. Feed this 5.00V into NodeMCU pin Vin (which utilizes the onboard AMS1117-3.3 regulator). Connecting 5V directly to the 3V3 pin will destroy the ESP8266 module instantly!
  • 4. SoftwareSerial Baud Alignment: Both Arduino Uno sketch and NodeMCU bridge sketch communicate at 9600 baud. This guarantees 100% stable packet reception on Uno's software bit-banging serial pins without dropped bytes.

Step-by-Step Hardware Verification Sequence
  1. Set and Verify 5.00V Buck Power: Disconnect Arduino and NodeMCU. Apply 12V DC input to LM2596. Use a digital multimeter on OUT+ and OUT- to verify exactly 5.00V ± 0.05V before plugging into breadboard power rails.
  2. Assemble the 1kΩ / 2kΩ Voltage Divider on Breadboard: Insert 1kΩ resistor between Row 15 and Row 20. Insert 2kΩ resistor between Row 20 and Row 25. Connect a blue jumper from Row 25 to the top blue Star Ground rail.
  3. Wire Arduino & NodeMCU Serial Lines: Connect Arduino Pin D10 to Breadboard Row 15. Connect Breadboard Row 20 (the 3.33V center tap) to NodeMCU Pin D1 (GPIO5 / RX). Connect NodeMCU Pin D2 (GPIO4 / TX) directly to Arduino Pin D9 (RX).
  4. Connect Star Power Rails: Run red jumper from Star 5V Rail to Arduino 5V and another to NodeMCU Vin. Run blue jumpers from Star GND Rail to Arduino GND and NodeMCU GND.
  5. Upload Firmware & Test Live: Upload wbacfspwi_arduino_controller.ino to Arduino Uno. Upload 09_esp8266_wifi_bridge_test.ino to NodeMCU via micro-USB. Open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud to observe telemetry packets transmitted wirelessly over WiFi.
