💧 HW-080 Inverted Resistance Behavior

Analog Reading (AO):
- Dry Surface (Air): High resistance → ADC reads ~850–1023 (0% flood).
- Standing Water (Ponding): Low resistance → ADC drops to ~250–450 (75%–100% flood).
When ponding depth reaches ≥ 75%, the controller automatically halts the pump to prevent miniature rice field flooding.
