⚡ Golden Rules for Error-Free Assembly

  • 1. Solar Charging & BMS Integration: Connect 30W Solar Panel outputs to the SOLAR IN+ and SOLAR IN- terminals of the 3S Solar Charge Controller / BMS. The BAT+ and BAT- outputs feed the 3S 18650 battery pack to provide regulated constant-current / constant-voltage charging up to 12.60V.
  • 2. Calibrate Buck Converter First: Disconnect Arduino and modules. Turn on 3S Battery power and adjust Buck potentiometer screw until multimeter reads 5.00V ± 0.05V before connecting to breadboard.
  • 3. Check 1N4007 Diode Stripe: The silver/white band (Cathode) MUST plug into Row 35 (switched 12V from relay). Connecting in reverse will short-circuit the relay!
  • 4. Star Ground Rule: Ensure all module grounds (Buck OUT-, Battery -, Solar Charger -, Solar Panel -, Arduino GND, Sensor GND, Relay GND) terminate on the blue breadboard rail to eliminate inductive voltage bounce.
  • 5. USB Data Bridge: Keep USB cable plugged into computer to view live serial telemetry (115200 baud) and web integration.
