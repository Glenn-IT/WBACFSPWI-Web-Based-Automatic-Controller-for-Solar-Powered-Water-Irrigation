WBACFSPWI Component Specifications

Arduino Uno R3 Microcontroller ATmega328P • 16MHz

Autonomous decision engine with 3-layer safety net: 40% refill threshold, 50% target shutoff, 5s anti-splash runtime cap, and 10s wave settling window.

30W Solar Panel & 3S MPPT/BMS Charger 18.0V Vmp • 12.6V CC/CV

Continuous solar harvesting with overcharge protection. 100kΩ/20kΩ voltage divider taps solar output into Arduino Pin A3.

LM2596 5V 3A Step-Down Buck Converter 12V IN ➔ 5.00V OUT

Efficient switching power supply driving Arduino Uno 5V, 5V Relay coil, NodeMCU Vin, and SIM900A GSM with zero thermal throttling.

5V Optocoupler Relay & 12V DC Pump Pin D7 Active LOW

Songle SRD-05VDC-SL-C switched relay clamped by a 1N4007 flyback diode across pump terminals to suppress inductive back-EMF spikes.

JSN-SR04T Waterproof Ultrasonic Sensor A1 (TRIG) • A4 (ECHO)

Non-contact acoustic depth measurement (20.0cm clearance, 8.0cm calibrated container depth) with median filtering to eliminate water surface ripple noise.

Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor v1.2 Pin D8 (Gate) • A0 (ADC)

Corrosion-resistant capacitive blade. Arduino Pin D8 provides switched power only during sampling to prevent electrochemical degradation.

NodeMCU ESP8266 & SIM900A GSM Dual Link WiFi Bridge + SMS Alerts

Real-time JSON telemetry streamed to XAMPP dashboard via ESP8266 (D9/D10) plus direct automated SMS event dispatches to Admin via SIM900A (D2/D3).

/*
 * WBACFSPWI — Full Standalone Arduino Uno Irrigation Controller
 * Web-Based Automatic Controller for Solar-Powered Water Irrigation
 * 
 * Target Application: Miniature Rice Field Automation
 * 
 * Hardware Setup:
 *   - Arduino Uno R3 (ATmega328P)
 *   - Pin A0: Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor v1.2 (Root Zone)
 *   - Pin A1: JSN-SR04T Waterproof Ultrasonic TRIG Output (10µs pulse, replaces legacy HW-080)
 *   - Pin A2: 3S 18650 / 12V Motorcycle Battery Voltage Divider (100kΩ / 33kΩ)
 *   - Pin A3: 30W Solar Panel Voltage Divider (100kΩ / 20kΩ)
 *   - Pin A4: JSN-SR04T Waterproof Ultrasonic ECHO Input (Time-of-Flight duration)
 *   - Pin D2: GSM SoftwareSerial RX (from SIM900A 5VT / TXD)
 *   - Pin D3: GSM SoftwareSerial TX (direct to SIM900A 5VR onboard level shifter)
 *   - Pin D7: 5V Relay Module (DC Water Pump Switch, Active LOW)
 *   - Pin D8: Soil Moisture Sensor Power Gate (Corrosion Prevention)
 *   - Pin D9: NodeMCU SoftwareSerial RX (from NodeMCU Pin D2 TX)
 *   - Pin D10: NodeMCU SoftwareSerial TX (to NodeMCU Pin D1 RX via 1kΩ / 2kΩ divider)
 *   - Pin D13: Status / Fault Indicator LED
 */

void setup() {
  Serial.begin(115200);

    digitalWrite(PIN_SENSOR_PWR, LOW);
  }

  espSerial.begin(9600);
  espSerial.listen();

Step-by-Step Bench Assembly Guide

  1. Pre-Calibrate LM2596 Buck Converter: Connect the 12V Battery to Buck IN+ and IN- with all other circuits disconnected. Turn the multi-turn trimpot brass screw until a digital multimeter reads 5.00V ± 0.05V across OUT+ and OUT-.
  2. Establish Breadboard Star Power Rails: Connect Buck OUT+ to the Breadboard top red rail (+) and Buck OUT- to the top blue rail (-). Tie Arduino GND, Battery (-), Solar (-), and Module grounds to this single central ground bus to avoid ground loops.
  3. Assemble Voltage Dividers:
    • Battery Monitor: 100kΩ R1 from Battery (+) to Row 10 tap, 33kΩ R2 from Row 10 tap to GND. Wire tap to Arduino Pin A2.
    • Solar Monitor: 100kΩ R3 from Solar (+) to Row 25 tap, 20kΩ R4 from Row 25 tap to GND. Wire tap to Arduino Pin A3.
    • ESP8266 Shifter: 1kΩ from Arduino D10 to tap, 2kΩ from tap to GND. Wire tap to NodeMCU Pin D1.
  4. Install 1N4007 Motor Flyback Diode: Insert diode across Row 35 and Row 40. The silver band (Cathode) MUST connect to Row 35 (switched 12V from relay NO). Connecting backwards will short-circuit the relay contacts!
  5. Wire Submersible DC Pump & 5V Relay: Connect 12V Battery (+) to Relay COM. Connect Relay NO to Breadboard Row 35. Connect Pump (+) lead to Row 35 and Pump (-) lead to Row 40 (Star GND). Connect Relay IN to Arduino Pin D7.
  6. Connect Dual Sensors: Connect Capacitive sensor VCC to Arduino Pin D8 (Power Gate), GND to Star GND, AOUT to Arduino Pin A0. Connect JSN-SR04T 5V to Star 5V, GND to Star GND, TRIG to Arduino A1, ECHO to Arduino A4.
  7. Connect Dual Telemetry Channels:
    • NodeMCU ESP8266: Vin to Star 5V, GND to Star GND, D2 (TX) to Arduino D9, D1 (RX) to 3.3V shifter tap.
    • SIM900A GSM: 5V supply to Buck 5V (2A burst), GND to Star GND, 5VT to Arduino D2, 5VR to Arduino D3.
