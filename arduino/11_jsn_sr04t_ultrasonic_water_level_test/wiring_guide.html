⚠️ Critical Installation Rule: The 25 cm Blind Zone Air Clearance

The JSN-SR04T transducer has a physical blind zone of 20 cm to 25 cm due to internal transducer ringing damping. To ensure accurate operation:

  • The transducer must be rigidly fastened at least 25 cm to 30 cm above the container's 100% full flood level.
  • This 25 cm zone must always remain air; water must never rise closer than 20 cm to the transducer face.
  • Ensure the downward acoustic cone ($45^\circ$ beam angle) is unobstructed by side walls, hoses, or pump cords.

Interactive Serial Monitor Calibration (115200 Baud)

1. Calibration Steps

  1. Flash 11_jsn_sr04t_ultrasonic_water_level_test.ino to your Arduino Uno.
  2. Open the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud.
  3. Step 1 (100% Full Mark): Fill container to target 100% flood mark. Press c. The sketch records this distance as sensorClearanceCM.
  4. Step 2 (0% Empty Mark): Drain container completely to floor. Press e. The sketch calculates containerDepthCM = measured - clearance.
  5. Step 3 (Diagnostic Burst): Press t to verify jitter is $\le 1.0\text{ cm}$.
