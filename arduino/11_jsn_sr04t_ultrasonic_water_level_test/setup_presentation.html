Three Golden Rules for Mounting in an 18 cm Circular Container
RULE 1: STRICT CENTERING

Center the Probe at Exactly 9.0 cm

Because your container is an 18 cm diameter circle, the radius is exactly 9.0 cm. The JSN-SR04T must be positioned directly in the geometric center.

Why: The sensor has a $\sim 50^\circ$ acoustic sound cone. If you mount it even 2 cm off-center, the sound waves will bounce off the curved sidewall before reaching the water surface!

RULE 2: HEIGHT CALCULATION

Transducer Height Above the Rim

The JSN-SR04T has a 20 cm blind zone.

• If you leave a 3 cm air gap inside the pot (so water doesn't spill over the top rim at 100% full):
• The transducer face should sit 18.0 cm above the top rim of the container ($18\text{cm} + 3\text{cm} = 21.0\text{cm}$ total clearance down to water).

RULE 3: SIMPLE CROSS-BRIDGE

Make a Simple Wood or Acrylic Bridge

Cut a 20 cm strip of wood, plastic ruler, or acrylic. Place it across the circular opening of the container with two small vertical risers (18 cm tall).

Drill a 20 mm hole in the center of the bridge and insert the JSN-SR04T probe pointing directly straight down into the center of the pot.
