⚠️ Critical SIM800L Power Warning

The SIM800L requires 3.7V to 4.4V (nominal 4.0V) and draws up to 2.0 Amperes peak burst current during cellular RF transmission. DO NOT attempt to power it from the Arduino 5V or 3.3V pin. Power it from the LM2596 Buck Converter or a 3.7V Li-ion battery, with a 1000μF capacitor across VCC and GND, and tie all GNDs together!

Step-by-Step Hardware Verification Sequence
  1. Insert Micro-SIM Card: Insert an activated SIM card with regular SMS load or promo. Ensure SIM PIN is disabled.
  2. Power Up & Observe NET LED: Apply power to the LM2596 buck converter and Arduino. The SIM800L NET LED will blink rapidly (1/sec) while searching for cellular signal, and slow down to 1 blink every 3 seconds once registered to the cell tower.
  3. Open Arduino IDE Serial Monitor: Set baud rate to 115200 baud. Type s to send an instant verification SMS to your phone.
  4. Live Water Level Simulation: Type 1 to simulate Trigger 1 (Irrigation Started <45%). Type 2 to simulate Trigger 2 (Irrigation Stopped ≥50%). Type 3 to simulate Trigger 3 (Irrigation Restarted <45% again).
