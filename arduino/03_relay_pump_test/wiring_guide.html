⚡ Star Wiring & Flyback Protection Architecture

Main Power: 3S Battery + Buck Converter (5.00V) powers the entire system.
Star Ground: Motor current flows strictly through Row 20 & 25 back to the Star GND point, completely bypassing the Arduino MCU to avoid resets or analog noise.
USB Connection: Used only as a Serial Data Bridge for web telemetry.
