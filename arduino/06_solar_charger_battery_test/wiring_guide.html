⚡ Golden Rules for Test 06 Assembly

  • 1. Calibrate Buck Converter First: Adjust LM2596 brass screw to 5.00V ± 0.05V before connecting to Arduino.
  • 2. Verify Voltage Dividers: Ensure 100k/33k (A2) and 100k/20k (A3) dividers are properly installed to prevent over-voltage damage to Arduino analog inputs.
  • 3. Serial Monitor Test: Upload 06_solar_charger_battery_test.ino and open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud to view real-time solar harvesting and battery state telemetry.
