📦 Prototype Bill of Materials (BOM)
  • 1x 12V/30W Polycrystalline Solar Panel with standard mounting bracket
  • 1x 10A/20A 12V PWM Solar Charge Controller with integrated LCD status screen
  • 1x 12V Motorcycle Lead-Acid or 3S 18650 Li-ion Battery Pack (11.1V–12.6V)
  • 1x 830-Point Solderless Breadboard with dual power distribution buses
  • 1x ESP32 NodeMCU-32S / ESP-WROOM-32 30-pin/38-pin Development Board
  • 1x Soil Moisture Sensor Module with LM393 comparator & dual-prong probe
  • 1x 5V/3.3V 1-Channel Optocoupler Relay Module (10A 250VAC / 30VDC rating)
  • 1x 12V DC Submersible/Diaphragm Miniature Water Pump with silicone tubing
  • Assorted Jumper Wires (Male-to-Male and Male-to-Female)
