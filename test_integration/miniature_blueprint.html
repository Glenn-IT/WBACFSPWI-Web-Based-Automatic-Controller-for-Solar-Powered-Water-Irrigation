Miniature Physical Assembly Steps
Step 1: Paddy Bed

Fill the bottom 5–7cm of the acrylic box with loam/clay soil. Insert the Capacitive Sensor (A0) vertically into the root zone. Mount the Surface Sensor (A1) 2cm above the soil to measure ponding depth.

Step 2: Water & Pump

Submerge the mini 12V pump into the water reservoir. Connect the 6mm silicone tubing from pump outlet and route it along the top edge of the rice paddy bed with micro-dripper / spray holes.

Step 3: Power & Arduino

Mount Arduino Uno, 3S 18650 Battery, LM2596 (set to 5.0V), and 5V Relay module in the control box. Connect common GND across all modules.
